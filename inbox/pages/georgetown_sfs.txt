News & Events
At the School of Foreign Service, we are committed to enhancing your academic experience at Georgetown
We offer a range of events in which you can engage with the subject of international affairs and special guests who impact the very issues you study.
We also keep you informed about the latest news from our faculty, students and alumni, and we highlight the expertise of our faculty on the most pressing issues of the day through research news, explainers and analysis.

Dean’s Coffee Chats
Coffee Chats provide a unique opportunity to engage directly with influential professionals and successful alumni who have made significant impacts in their fields. During these informal gatherings, you can ask questions about their experiences and gain valuable insights.
Explore previous guests <https://sfs.georgetown.edu/news-events/coffee-chats/>
Diplomatic Ball
The Diplomatic Ball is an annual black-tie event uniting our students, alumni, faculty and members of the diplomatic community. Taking place every spring at a prestigious location in Washington, DC, the Diplomatic Ball is one of the most highly anticipated events of the year.
Learn about the Diplomatic Ball <https://sfs.georgetown.edu/news-events/diplomatic-ball>
