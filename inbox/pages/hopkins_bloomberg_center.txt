### Stories That Matter Presents "The Cycle of Love"
Link: https://hub.jhu.edu/events/2026/11/30/stories-that-matter-film-screening-the-cycle-of-love/
Published: 
When: November 30th 6:00pm - 8:30pm
Where: Hopkins Bloomberg Center
Stories that Matter presents a special screening of 
The Cycle of Love
, an inspiring, heartwarming and epic true-life adventure directed by Oscar-winning filmmaker Orlando von Einsiedel (
The White Helmets
). In the film, a young Indian street artist embarks on an extraordinary 6,000-mile bicycle journey from India to Sweden in 1977 to demonstrate his love and devotion. The film explores the depth of love, the courage to overcome social and cultural barriers, and what it means to risk everything for what your heart tells you.
The screening will be followed by a panel discussion featuring director von Einsiedel and Anand Pandian, the Krieger-Eisenhower Professor of Anthropology and Director of Graduate Studies at the Johns Hopkins Krieger School of Arts and Sciences. The discussion will be moderated by Sig Libowitz, director of the Johns Hopkins Graduate Film and Media Program.
