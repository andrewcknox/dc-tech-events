Pop-Up Conversation with Rep. Josh Gottheimer
Tuesday, November 17, 2026
•
9 a.m. ET
•
Washington, D.C.
Join Punchbowl News for a conversation with Rep. Josh Gottheimer (D-N.J.) on Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 9 a.m. ET. Punchbowl News Tech Reporter Anthony Adragna will sit down with Gottheimer to discuss how Washington and the private sector can work together to verify real people, stop bad actors and deliver safer, easier experiences that work for Americans. Afterward, Caryn Seidman Becker, chair and CEO of CLEAR, will join Punchbowl News Founder and CEO Anna Palmer for a fireside chat.
