Crypto Summit: Washington, D.C.
Tuesday, December 1, 2026
•
8 a.m. ET
•
Washington, D.C.
Join Punchbowl News on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 8 a.m. ET for a summit on the future of cryptocurrency. Punchbowl News Founder and CEO Anna Palmer will sit down with Sen.-elect Andy Barr (R-Ky.) to discuss what’s next for crypto policy and how Congress can secure financial systems while also protecting consumers and safeguarding innovation. Following the discussion, we’ll sit down for a crypto advocate panel moderated by Punchbowl News Senior Financial Services Reporter, Brendan Pedersen.
